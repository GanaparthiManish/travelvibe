Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 21, 2026 | Applies to TravelVibe Mobile App & TravelVibe Agent App

1. Introduction

Welcome to TravelVibe. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data. This Privacy Policy describes how TravelVibe ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, stores, and protects information gathered from users of our mobile applications (TravelVibe and TravelVibe Agent) and related services.

2. Information We Collect

We collect several types of information from and about users of our apps:

  • Personal Identification Data: Full name, email address, phone number, and profile pictures when you create an account or sign in using Google Auth or email authentication.
  • Agent & Guide Verification Data (TravelVibe Agent App): Government-issued identity documents, tourism licenses, and bank/UPI payout details for identity verification and payout processing.
  • Location Data: Precise or approximate location information (GPS) with your consent to connect travelers with nearby verified local tour guides and show real-time itinerary routes.
  • Booking & Financial Data: Tour reservation history, preferences, and payment receipts processed via secure payment gateways.
  • Device & Usage Data: IP address, device type, operating system version, and app performance logs to improve application reliability.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information for the following core purposes:

  • To facilitate tour bookings, guide discovery, and real-time chat between travelers and local guides.
  • To verify local guide credentials and prevent fraudulent activity.
  • To process secure payments and disburse guide earnings via payment channels.
  • To provide customer support and respond to inquiries.
  • To send booking updates, service notifications, and security alerts.

4. Data Sharing & Third-Party Services

We do not sell your personal data. We may share data with trusted service providers strictly to perform essential application functions:

  • Authentication & Database Services: Supabase and Google Sign-In for secure authentication and encrypted data storage.
  • Payment Processors: Razorpay / Stripe for processing payments and guide payouts securely.
  • Legal Requirements: If required by law, subpoena, or government authority to protect the rights, safety, or property of TravelVibe or our users.

5. Data Security & Retention

We implement industry-standard encryption (SSL/TLS in transit and AES encryption at rest) to safeguard your personal data. Your data is retained only for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes outlined in this policy or to comply with statutory legal obligations.

6. Your Rights & Data Deletion

You have the right to access, update, or request the deletion of your personal data at any time. To request account and data deletion:

  • Navigate to Settings > Account > Delete Account within the TravelVibe or TravelVibe Agent mobile app.
  • Or send an email to privacy@travelvibe.app with the subject "Data Deletion Request". We will process your request within 7 business days.
